# Instagram carousel production report

## Deliverables

- `01.jpg` — cover and Brisbane backyard clay-pipe cutaway
- `02.jpg` — three-stage root-entry mechanism
- `03.jpg` — fictional plumber and homeowner reviewing a drain-camera image
- `04.jpg` — three-sign checklist and save CTA

All four files are RGB PNGs at 1120 × 1400 pixels, an exact portrait 4:5 ratio.

## Tool and model

The images were generated and edited with Codex's built-in image generation tool. The tool did not expose a specific image-model identifier, so no model name or version is claimed here.

## Art direction

The deck uses a consistent technical field-guide direction for a trade business: warm cream and concrete neutrals, terracotta clay, deep bottle green, and a single safety-orange CTA accent. Photoreal cutaways and a candid human scene carry the explanation; there is no business name, logo, phone number, statistic, testimonial, comment keyword, or invented resource.

## Inspection performed

Each final was viewed at original size and again from a 430-pixel-wide copy.

- Copy: checked the approved headline, support line, labels, checklist items, punctuation, and CTA against `BRIEF.md`.
- Readability: checked headline and label legibility at phone width, margins, text collisions, and hierarchy.
- Teaching value: checked that roots visibly enter the old clay joint, the three stages read in order, the monitor shows roots inside a clay drain, and each checklist icon supports its line.
- People: checked slide 3's eyes, hands, fingers, grip, pointing gesture, gaze, body proportions, and contact with the monitor.
- Composition: checked label pointers, protected faces and hands, consistent palette, and interior-slide finish.
- File properties: checked PNG format and center-cropped the generated 1122 × 1402 files by one pixel per edge to exact 1120 × 1400 output.

Automated OCR was also attempted as a secondary check. It read some of the large copy but missed several condensed display lines, so visual inspection of the actual images was the deciding check.

## Targeted fixes

- Slide 1: reduced and repositioned the headline block to improve top and right safe margins while preserving the scene, labels, and wording.
- Slide 2: corrected the middle-stage root path so the roots funnel through the clay joint seam rather than appearing to pass through solid pipe wall.
- Slide 3: removed a tiny generated yellow equipment label from the drain-camera reel; faces, hands, monitor, and composition were preserved.
- Slide 4: reduced and repositioned the headline for a safer top margin. That edit introduced a dark top-border artifact, so a second targeted edit replaced only the artifact with the existing cream background texture.

## Remaining imperfections

- The cutaways and drain-camera view are educational illustrations, not engineering drawings or a diagnosis of a particular property.
- The display lettering is generated typography, not a named or supplied brand font.
- Slide 2 simplifies root growth into three clean stages; the mechanism is intentionally clearer than a real excavation would be.
- Slide 3's people are fictional illustrative subjects, not real staff or customers.

No blocking spelling, anatomy, pointer, collision, or phone-readability defect remained after the final inspection.
